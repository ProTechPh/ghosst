Welcome to Ghosst. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to deliver application features, manage key redemptions, and prevent fraudulent activities:

  • Account Information: If you sign in or create an account, we may store your user identifier and profile display name managed through our backend (Appwrite).
  • Virtual Currency & In-App Activity: Virtual coins earned via rewarded ads, transaction/claim records, and redeemed product keys.
  • Device & Usage Data: Diagnostic logs, basic device specifications, and crash data to ensure optimal stability.

2. Third-Party Services and Advertising

Our app integrates third-party services that may collect information used to identify you or personalize ads in accordance with their respective privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob: We show ads (including rewarded ads). Google AdMob may use device identifiers (like Advertising ID) and cookies to serve ads based on your visits. For details, check Google Privacy & Terms.
  • Appwrite: Used for secure cloud database authentication, user data sync, and server-side verification (SSV).

3. Permissions Requested

Depending on your usage, the app may request the following permissions:

  • Internet Access: To synchronize coin balances, authenticate, retrieve products, and load ad networks.
  • Install Packages (REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES): Only needed if you choose to download and install official APK updates or product files directly through the app.

4. How We Use Information

We use collected data solely to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Ghosst app services.
  • Credit coins earned through rewarded advertising (using Server-Side Verification).
  • Facilitate the delivery of software keys and files.
  • Prevent fraud, botting, and abuse of the rewards system.

5. Data Retention & Security

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your personal information. We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal information to third parties.

6. Children's Privacy

Our application is not targeted towards children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be posted directly to this URL with an updated "Last Updated" date.
