Delete your Ghosst account

Developer: AstrixTech · Package: com.astrixtech.ghosst

Delete inside the app

  1. Sign in to Ghosst.
  2. Open your profile.
  3. Select Delete account, type DELETE, and confirm.

This is the fastest method and permanently deletes the account immediately.

Request deletion without the app

Email us from the same email address registered to your Ghosst account. Use the subject Ghosst account deletion request and include the account email in the message.

Email deletion request

We may ask you to verify account ownership. Verified requests are completed within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Appwrite authentication account and active sessions
  • Email address, profile, and coin balance
  • Reward history and purchase/claim records

Operational inventory records may remain after your user identifier has been removed from them. We do not retain a copy of your deleted profile for marketing.
